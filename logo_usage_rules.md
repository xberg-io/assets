# Xberg Logo Usage Rules

## Spelling lock

- The wordmark text is `berg` only.
- Because the symbol represents the X, the complete name reads Xberg.

## Use

- Use Rotated Junction V5 as the primary symbol.
- Use the Primary Wordmark when the name must be visible.
- Preserve the Original Junction as an additional brand asset.

## Do not

- Do not redraw or reinterpret option 5.
- Do not change the `berg` typography without approval.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or alter spacing without approval.
